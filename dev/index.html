BetaDispersion.jl

BetaDispersion tests whether groups differ in their spread in a dissimilarity space. It follows vegan::betadisper and its permutation test. A significant result can help distinguish differences in dispersion from differences in group location when interpreting PERMANOVA.

Quick start

using BetaDispersion

# D is a square dissimilarity matrix; group has one label per row of D.
bd = betadisper(D, group)
permutest(bd; permutations=999, pairwise=true)
dispersion_anova(bd)

Pass observations and a Distances.jl metric instead with betadisper(X, group, BrayCurtis()), where rows of X are observations. The default group centre is the spatial median; use type=:centroid for centroids.

BetaDispersion.BetaDisper — Type
BetaDisper

Result of betadisper.

  • distances: distance of each observation to its group's centre
  • group, levels: group of each observation, and the sorted group levels
  • group_distances: mean distance to centre in each group (in levels order)
  • centroids: group centres (spatial medians or centroids), one row per level, in the principal coordinate space
  • vectors, eig: principal coordinates of the observations and their eigenvalues (zero eigenvalues removed; negative ones kept, as in vegan)
  • type: :median or :centroid
  • labels: observation names (may be empty)

BetaDispersion.DispersionTest — Type
DispersionTest

Result of permutest or dispersion_anova: a one-way ANOVA of the distances to group centres.

  • df, sumsq, meansq: for groups and residuals
  • F, p: the F statistic and its p-value (permutation or F distribution)
  • permutations: number of permutations (0 for the parametric test)
  • pairwise: nothing, or a named tuple of vectors group1, group2, t, p (two-sided permutation p-value), p_adjusted and p_parametric (pooled-variance t-test)
  • adjust: the multiple-testing correction applied to p_adjusted

BetaDispersion.betadisper — Function
betadisper(D, group; type=:median, bias_adjust=false, sqrt_dist=false,
           add=false, labels=String[])
betadisper(X, group, metric; kw...)

Distances of observations to their group centre in principal coordinate space (Anderson 2006), the same as vegan::betadisper(d, group).

D is a symmetric dissimilarity matrix; or give a data matrix X (one row per observation) and a Distances.jl metric such as BrayCurtis(). type=:centroid uses group centroids instead of spatial medians. add=:lingoes or :cailliez corrects non-Euclidean dissimilarities; sqrt_dist=true takes square roots first. bias_adjust=true corrects for small group sizes. Observations whose group is missing are dropped.

Test the differences with permutest or dispersion_anova.

source
BetaDispersion.permutest — Function
permutest(bd::BetaDisper; permutations=999, pairwise=false, adjust=:holm,
          rng=Random.default_rng())

Permutation test of differences in dispersion between groups. The F statistic of a one-way ANOVA of the distances to group centres is compared with its distribution when the ANOVA residuals are permuted (as vegan's permutest.betadisper). P-values are (1 + #{F_perm ≥ F}) / (1 + permutations).

With pairwise=true each pair of groups is also compared with a two-sided permutation t-test (distances permuted between the two groups only), and the p-values are corrected for multiple testing: adjust is :holm (default), :bh (Benjamini-Hochberg), :bonferroni or :none.

permutations may also be a matrix with one permutation of 1:n per row.
